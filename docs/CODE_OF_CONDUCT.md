# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).

## Our pledge

Participation must be harassment-free and welcoming to people of every background and level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give and accept feedback in good faith.
- Focus discussion on the work and its impact.
- Respect privacy and personal boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, sexualized content or attention, and sharing private information without permission are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when conduct violates these standards. Report an incident privately through a maintainer's GitHub profile or the repository's [private reporting channel](SECURITY.md). Reports are handled discreetly and fairly.

The full policy and enforcement guidance are maintained by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
